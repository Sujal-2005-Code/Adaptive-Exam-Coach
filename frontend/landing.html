🎓 SmartPrep AI Interactive Walkthrough

Here is how our adaptive engine pinpoints weaknesses and helps you master your exams:

⚡ Real-Time Adaptive Logic:

  • Weighting Formula: Each topic starts equal, but as you practice, the system calculates weight = max(0.12, 1 - accuracy) + 0.15.
  • Micro-Topic Targeting: Drills into specific weak units (e.g., Kinematics inside Physics).
  • Dynamic Difficulty: Correct answers elevate you from Easy to Hard; mistakes gently reduce difficulty to reinforce fundamentals.
  • Socratic AI Tutor: Never hands out direct answers during tests; guides you with conceptual hints and diagrams.
